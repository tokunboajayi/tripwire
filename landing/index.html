Three brains and a shield

Calm → Mean-ReversionBuys sharp dips, sells the bounce.
Trending → TrendRides the move, sized by volatility.
Volatile → BreakoutBuys confirmed breakouts, tight trailing stop.
Falling → ShieldHolds cash until the market turns.

AI picks the brain → Rust verifies every order in microseconds → you watch, with a reason for every move

The AI can tighten your limits. It can never loosen them. If the AI is slow or wrong, Rust falls back to its own rules.
